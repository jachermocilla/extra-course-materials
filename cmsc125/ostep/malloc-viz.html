Watching malloc() and free() work

A 4KB heap starting at virtual address 16KB, following the Free-Space Management chapter of OSTEP. Every allocated chunk begins with an 8-byte header: 4 bytes for its size and 4 bytes for a magic number. The allocator uses first fit, puts freed chunks at the front of the free list, and never coalesces.
